============================================================
                    ABOUT THE PROJECT
============================================================

Sengol Shopping is a modern shopping discovery and affiliate
platform created under the Sengol brand.

The platform is designed to help users discover products across
different categories and marketplaces through a clean, modern,
mobile-friendly and scalable interface.

Sengol Shopping is currently under active development and is
planned to grow into a larger shopping ecosystem.


============================================================
                    PROJECT VISION
============================================================

The long-term vision of Sengol Shopping is to create a scalable
digital shopping ecosystem where users can:

• Discover products
• Explore categories
• Compare products
• Find trending products
• Save favourite products
• Review products
• Follow product collections
• Visit original marketplaces
• Discover offers and deals

The platform may gradually expand into additional Sengol
products and services in the future.

                    SUPPORT

Sengol Shopping may provide a voluntary support option through

============================================================
                    AFFILIATE MODEL
============================================================

Sengol Shopping may use authorized affiliate links where
applicable.

Users may be redirected to an original marketplace, seller or
product page to complete a purchase.

Sengol Shopping may receive a commission from qualifying
purchases where an authorized affiliate relationship exists.

Affiliate relationships should be disclosed clearly to users.
